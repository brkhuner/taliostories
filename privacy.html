1. Introduction

Welcome to Talio! We are fully committed to protecting your personal information and your children's privacy. Talio is designed to provide a safe, magical, and educational reading experience for families. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application.

2. Children's Privacy & Safety

As an app designed for families, children's safety is our absolute priority. We comply with all applicable children's privacy regulations, including COPPA:

  • We do not collect or request any personal identifiers from child users.
  • Reading progress, unlocked badges, and avatar selections are stored locally on your device and synced securely with an encrypted Firebase database associated with an anonymous device identifier (or optional registered account).
  • There are no public forums, social sharing features, or unmoderated chat functionalities in Talio. Your child cannot communicate with anyone inside the app.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information to provide our services:

  • No personal identifiers (such as email addresses, names, or phone numbers) are collected or required to use the app. Registration is entirely optional or bypassed;
  • Basic anonymous app-wide metrics (such as the number of books finished, reading streaks) to maintain user progress and synced stats;
  • Dynamic theme choices and font preference settings selected inside the app.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy or our child safety commitments, please reach out to us directly at taliostories.support@gmail.com.
